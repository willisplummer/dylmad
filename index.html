~ Everything You Need to Know ~

Where is the wedding?
The wedding is located at our lovely friend's farm, just outside of Eugene, near Creswell. The address is 84674 Cloverdale Rd, Creswell, OR 97426. You will need to drive to this location, then follow signs towards the river to arrive at the gathering space.
Where do I fly into?
Eugene, OR. You can also fly into Portland and drive or take the train to Eugene (about two hours).
Where do I stay?
Please come camp on the farm! You can come set up your stuff at any point starting Friday morning. There is plenty of space for tents or RVs/trailers. No RV hookups. For those wanting something a little more comfortable we've also reserved a few rooms around town: In downtown Eugene, there's the Campbell House, a historic boutique hotel: https://www.campbellhouse.com There are only a few rooms left so look online and then call the hotel at (541-343-1119); you can tell the front desk that you are with the Plummer-Cowen wedding and ask for the room you would like to book. In south Eugene, closer to Creswell, there's the Comfort Suites Eugene: https://www.choicehotels.com/oregon/eugene/comfort-suites-hotels/or163 We have a group rate of $309 king bed and $319 two queens. Ask for the Cowen plummer wedding party. The rate is plus tax and includes breakfast parking and wi-fi. There is a required 2 night minimum length of stay for the weekend.
How do I get to and from the wedding?
Carpooling is cool! Please don't drink & drive! You can take taxis, ubers, lyfts to and from the farm. We recommend pre-scheduling a ride back to where you're staying (or just stay on the farm!).
Can we bring our kids?
Yes, bring the kiddos! We will have a designated quiet camp for families. Please no dogs unless you absolutely need to.
What's the dress code?
Country western or whimsical farm fairy attire is encouraged but wear whatever makes you feel celebratory. Terrain is rugged and uneven so comforatable supportive shoes are are recommended.
